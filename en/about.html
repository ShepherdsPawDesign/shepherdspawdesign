About me

IkoDesigner / Illustrator

Through illustration and design, I bring small stories into everyday life. I create visuals tailored to each purpose, including illustrations, logos, and printed materials.

Based in Canada, I work with the graphic design knowledge I developed at BCIT (British Columbia Institute of Technology). I especially enjoy working with plants and animals as motifs.

My experience as a teacher informs the way I collaborate, and I am passionate about disability, inclusive design, and LGBTQIA2S+ related themes.

I would love to help shape your ideas into visuals that feel true to you. My motto is thoughtful, careful work from the first conversation to the final piece.
